This is Calle 5

Smoke runs
in the family.

We’re a family-owned Arizona taco truck built around a simple idea: take the patience of American barbecue and hit it with the energy of Mexican street food.

Every event gets the good stuff—smoked meats, bold salsas, warm tortillas, and a crew that knows how to feed a crowd. From neighborhood nights to packed brewery patios, Calle 5 brings people together around the fire.
